## The output standard (page 5)

Each clause as the package states it (paraphrased), how this repository implements it, and the gaps
found on `test/corpus-tasks` (2026-10-02).

### 1. Reliability and attribution

> Every shar'i statement, quotation or ruling shown can be traced to its source; nothing is attributed to a reference that does not contain it; the shar'i text is kept apart from generated explanation; the reply says when the information is insufficient.

Implemented:

- Faith questions are answered only from the corpus, never from model memory; a faith topic never reaches the chat persona, and the persona never sees retrieved passages. ([`doc/conversation-policy.md`](../../doc/conversation-policy.md), [`src/companion_api/rag/service.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/service.py), [`src/companion_api/rag/chat.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/chat.py))
- The faith prompt allows only the numbered sources, a citation after every sentence, third-person narration, and quotations copied word for word inside « ». ([`src/companion_api/rag/prompts.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/prompts.py))
- grounding-v3 releases an answer only when every sentence is attributed to a cited passage that holds most of its words, and refuses a quotation that is not word for word in a cited passage (`misquoted`). ([`src/companion_api/rag/grounding.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/grounding.py))
- The faith judge withholds an answer whose cited passages tell a different scene or teaching or do not say what the answer says; any "no", unreadable verdict or error withholds it. ([`src/companion_api/rag/judge.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/judge.py))
- Every answer returns its sources (passage id, title, work and reference). Sacred text is copied verbatim from sha256-pinned registered sources, and a hadith document must carry a grading. ([`src/companion_api/rag/service.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/service.py), [`corpus/sources/registry.yaml`](../../corpus/sources/registry.yaml), [`scripts/fetch_sources.py`](../../scripts/fetch_sources.py), [`src/companion_api/rag/corpus.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/corpus.py))
- With nothing adequate, Robert says he has no checked lesson about it and refers the child to a parent, a teacher or a qualified local scholar (`ABSTAIN_FAITH`). ([`src/companion_api/rag/responses.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/responses.py))

Gaps:

- The sources returned with an answer carry the work and reference but not the hadith's grading, which stays in the passage metadata; the glossary rule for الحديث ("state its grading when used as evidence") is not met in the reply.
- Generated retelling is told apart from quoted text only by the « » marks and the source list; nothing labels the retelling as generated (the app's display was not checked here).
- The judge is the same model that wrote the answer. On 2026-10-01 three wrong answers passed it (`doc/pr/test-corpus-tasks.md`).

### 2. The settled and the disputed (qat'i and ijtihad)

> Disputed matters are never presented as settled; a difference is mentioned as far as the context needs, without overwhelming the reader.

Implemented:

- Ruling, madhhab and sect questions are redirected before any model call (English, Arabic, Arabizi). ([`src/companion_api/rag/router.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/router.py), [`src/companion_api/rag/arabic_rules.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/arabic_rules.py))
- Fiqh differences, creed controversies and sectarian topics are outside the corpus's scope; a family's madhhab is never inferred. ([`doc/governance/scope.md`](../../doc/governance/scope.md), [`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md))
- A corpus document records whether its matter is common or differs (`madhhabScope`). ([`src/companion_api/rag/corpus.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/corpus.py))

Gaps:

- `madhhabScope` is recorded but not used: retrieval filters by language and age band only, and no reply says that scholars differ (src/companion_api/rag/retriever.py).
- The board's policy for recognised differences is undecided (doc/architecture.md §6.5; scope.md, decisions needed).

### 3. No independent fatwa

> No personal fatwa or ruling on a matter that needs the facts or specialist judgement; refer, or ask for clarification.

Implemented:

- The router's ruling category (halal and haram, fatwa, the validity of worship, "will Allah accept", madhhab) gives the fixed RULING reply, which names a qualified scholar or a trusted grown-up. ([`src/companion_api/rag/router.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/router.py), [`src/companion_api/rag/arabic_rules.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/arabic_rules.py), [`src/companion_api/rag/responses.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/responses.py))
- Both prompts forbid rulings and authority claims; output checks refuse "my fatwa", "I am a scholar" and the like (`authority_claim`). ([`src/companion_api/rag/prompts.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/prompts.py), [`src/companion_api/rag/grounding.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/grounding.py))
- Robert is not an imam, a scholar, a mufti or a teacher of rulings. ([`doc/robert-persona.md`](../../doc/robert-persona.md))

Gaps:

- Detection is by keywords. A personal case asked without ruling words reaches the faith path (which answers only from a verified passage); on 2026-10-01, 1 of 25 ruling questions abstained instead of being redirected.
- Level د asks for general information and a referral; Robert gives the referral only.

### 4. Resisting hallucination

> With no adequate reference or low confidence, abstain, hedge or refer rather than give an undocumented answer.

Implemented:

- Weak retrieval evidence gives the faith abstention before any generation; the model replies NOT_IN_SOURCES when the passages do not answer; any verification failure, judge "no" or error abstains. ([`src/companion_api/rag/retriever.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/retriever.py), [`src/companion_api/rag/service.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/service.py), [`src/companion_api/rag/prompts.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/prompts.py))
- Only immutable releases are served; a candidate or rejected source never enters a release, and the published channel needs approved content from cleared sources. ([`src/companion_api/rag/release.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/release.py), [`doc/governance/releases.md`](../../doc/governance/releases.md))

Gaps:

- Support is checked lexically and meaning by the same model; there is no reranker; abstention is measured on synthetic questions only.

### 5. Da'wah quality

> Consider the reader's background, level, language and context; foundations before details; correct, clear and well presented, without harmful oversimplification.

Implemented:

- Answers for ages 7 to 11 in at most three short sentences of simple words; respectful and gentle on faith, with no jokes. ([`src/companion_api/rag/prompts.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/prompts.py), [`doc/robert-persona.md`](../../doc/robert-persona.md))
- No frightening detail, no isra'iliyyat, no depiction of prophets, companions or angels. ([`doc/governance/scope.md`](../../doc/governance/scope.md))
- Age-band versions of each document are drafted for review. ([`scripts/age_band.py`](../../scripts/age_band.py))
- Invitations to a faith lesson carry no guilt, pressure or reward. ([`doc/conversation-policy.md`](../../doc/conversation-policy.md), [`src/companion_api/rag/responses.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/responses.py))

Gaps:

- No child content has been reviewed yet (layer 2 is templates and drafts), and the curriculum order ("foundations before details") is an open decision (doc/architecture.md §18).

### 6. Translation and localisation

> Keep the shar'i meaning of terms; respect the cultural context without changing the content to please the audience.

Implemented:

- Replies are given in the child's language (Arabic for Arabic script and Arabizi); faith answers come from the Arabic corpus, and English ones would need licensed, reviewed translations. ([`src/companion_api/rag/service.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/service.py), [`doc/conversation-policy.md`](../../doc/conversation-policy.md))
- The cross-lingual glossary records each term's Jamharah dictionary entry and, for the package's ten sample terms, the package's English equivalent and usage rule. ([`corpus/glossary_cross_lingual.yaml`](../../corpus/glossary_cross_lingual.yaml), [`scripts/crawl_jamharah.py`](../../scripts/crawl_jamharah.py))

Gaps:

- The glossary only rewrites Arabizi searches; it does not yet govern the wording of answers.
- Where Jamharah's English heading differs from the package's equivalent, the board has to choose; the package's is recorded as the one to use.
- All Arabic copy awaits review by native speakers.

### 7. Transparency

> Disclose that this is an AI-supported tool when the user may think they are dealing with a human specialist.

Implemented:

- Robert is a robot character who never claims to be a person; chat replies that do are refused (`human_claim`). ([`doc/robert-persona.md`](../../doc/robert-persona.md), [`src/companion_api/rag/chat.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/chat.py))
- The fixed replies say he answers faith questions only from lessons his teachers checked and that he is not a scholar. ([`src/companion_api/rag/responses.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/responses.py))

Gaps:

- No reply or notice says that answers are produced by an AI model; a robot character is not the same disclosure. A parent-facing notice is not in this repository.

### 8. Privacy

> Collect personal or sensitive data only as needed and under a stated policy; never draw unnecessary religious conclusions about the user.

Implemented:

- Personal data (names, addresses, schools, phone numbers, passwords) is redirected before any model call, and the persona never asks for personal details (`personal_details`). ([`src/companion_api/rag/router.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/router.py), [`src/companion_api/rag/responses.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/responses.py), [`src/companion_api/rag/chat.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/chat.py))
- Logs carry versions and outcome codes only, never the question, passages or answer; the small-talk intent (a feeling) is never logged; a turn stores only the finished answer. ([`src/companion_api/rag/service.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/service.py), [`doc/conversation-policy.md`](../../doc/conversation-policy.md), [`src/companion_api/store.py`](../../src/companion_api/store.py))
- No child content reaches the vector store, and a family's madhhab is never inferred. ([`src/companion_api/rag/release.py`](../../src/companion_api/rag/release.py), [`tests/test_no_child_content.py`](../../tests/test_no_child_content.py), [`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md))

Gaps:

- There is no written privacy policy in this repository ("a stated policy"); consent, retention and deletion flows are a Phase 0 gate (doc/architecture.md §8, §14), and the development store is an in-memory prototype (doc/development-boundary.md).

| ج: Disputed or highly sensitive | Fiqh differences, detailed creed, contested history and questions that need a specialist. A restricted answer from approved material, or a statement that a difference exists, or a referral. | Ruling, madhhab and sect questions take the ruling route (`redirected`, RULING). Other disputed topics are outside the corpus (doc/governance/scope.md), so the faith path abstains with a referral. | `redirected`, `abstained` | No reply says that a difference exists; detection rests on keywords or on the corpus not holding the topic. |
| د: Fatwa or a personal case | A ruling on an individual case, the validity of a person's contract or worship, family disputes, legal or medical matters with a shar'i effect. No independent ruling; general information and a referral. | Validity and "will Allah accept" patterns take the ruling route (`redirected`); distress, abuse and parents fighting take the safety routes (SAFETY_DISTRESS, SAFETY_ABUSE); personal data is redirected. | `redirected`, `safety` | A referral only, with no general information. |
| out of scope: Outside the package's scope | Personal fatwas, judging persons or groups, private disputes, rulings built on unverified individual facts. | Fatwas and sects take the ruling route; private disputes reach the safety distress reply when a distress pattern matches. | `redirected`, `safety`, `abstained` | There is no fixed route for judging a person or a group ("is my uncle a kafir?"); it is a faith topic and goes to the faith path, which answers only when a passage and the judge verify it. |

## Open questions for the organizers

- **Q1. Tafsir outside the rule as written.** The tafsir rule admits sources of the first three Hijri centuries or dorar.net/tafseer. Tafsir Ibn Kathir (d. 774 AH) and al-Tafsir al-Muyassar (King Fahd Complex, 1419 AH) are in the development corpus (candidate), and al-Sa'di and al-Mukhtasar were downloaded for comparison. May any of them be used, for example al-Muyassar's plain meaning for children, or must they stay out of every release? Sources: `ibn-kathir-ar-001..114`, `alquran-cloud-muyassar`, `quranpedia-tafsir-saadi`, `quranpedia-tafsir-mukhtasar`.
- **Q2. Borderline tafsir.** Does "the first three centuries" admit al-Tabari (d. 310 AH) and al-Nasa'i's tafsir (d. 303 AH), whose authors died early in the fourth century but transmit the earlier generations' reports? Does it admit al-Sahih al-Masbur (1420 AH), a modern compilation of graded early reports? Is the English al-Mukhtasar, listed on quranpedia.net as a translation, covered by the Quran row's "translations on quranpedia.net"? Sources: `quranpedia-tafsir-tabari`, `quranpedia-tafsir-nasai`, `quranpedia-tafsir-sahih-masbur`, `quranpedia-en-mukhtasar`.
- **Q3. Access to Dorar's data.** dorar.net refuses scripted requests (Cloudflare 403), so its tafsir, hadith, creed and ethics encyclopedias cannot be fetched; we do not work around the block. Can the organizers provide exports or arrange them with Dorar? The request is drafted in doc/governance/data-request.md.
- **Q4. Quranpedia's licence.** Quranpedia's licence says that translations and contemporary works remain the property of their authors and publishers. Does the organizers' permission cover the three English translations, al-Mukhtasar (Arabic and English), al-Sa'di and al-Sahih al-Masbur? The licence also asks every copy to be kept current (/api/v1/changes); may a release pin one reviewed dump version? Sources: `quranpedia-en-hilali-khan`, `quranpedia-en-sahih-international`, `quranpedia-en-ruwwad`, `quranpedia-en-mukhtasar`, `quranpedia-tafsir-mukhtasar`, `quranpedia-tafsir-saadi`, `quranpedia-tafsir-sahih-masbur`.
- **Q5. The permission in writing.** The project owner reported the organizers' permission to use the package's sources on 2026-10-02. Please confirm it in writing, so that it can be recorded with the governance decisions.
- **Q6. Hadith outside the two Sahihs.** An-Nawawi's Forty, Riyad as-Salihin and al-Adab al-Mufrad have no grading in our data. Is a grading from Dorar's hadith encyclopedia, with the grader's name, the "approved grading" the package requires? Which numbering of Sahih Muslim should references use (Abdul-Baqi's)? The development corpus displays Tanzil's Uthmani text (Hafs), which is to be cross-checked against Quranpedia's Hafs text. Is Tanzil acceptable on screen once the two agree, or should the displayed text be Quranpedia's? Sources: `tanzil-quran-uthmani`, `tanzil-quran-simple`, `quranpedia-mushaf-hafs`.

For التوحيد Jamharah gives "Monotheism", while the package gives "Tawhid / Oneness of God" and warns against a translation that suggests mere numerical oneness; for الدعوة Jamharah gives "Invitation" (the package "Da'wah / Invitation to Islam"); for السنة Jamharah's entry defines the fiqh sense (an act rewarded when done), the package the Prophet's guidance and way.
