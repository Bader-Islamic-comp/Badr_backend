## What a reviewer should check first

1. `STORY_IN` comes from the source maps under review (`doc/decisions/04-content-review.md`).
2. `MENTIONS_PROPHET` from hadith: names after narrator words or before بن are skipped as narrators, but a name used for a companion or a later person can still slip through.
3. `QUOTES_AYAH` and `CITES_HADITH` by text are n-gram matches: a shared formula can link the wrong ayah or hadith. `coverage` says how much of the ayah or hadith was found.
4. Ambiguous names (هود، صالح، يحيى، أحمد) are linked only with a context word; check the misses.
